# Conformance cases

Test cases every implementation of the light rules (`docs/board/light-v1.md`) must pass. The native tests run them against the light engine (`test/test_conformance`).

Expected values are worked out by hand from the specification, with a calculator for the formulas. They are never generated by running the engine, the firmware or the simulator.

## Light

- `light/curve.json`: lightness to duty.
- `light/power.json`: the power limit on given duties, in channel order red, green, blue, white.
- `light/record.json`: the stored record of the light settings, encoded and decoded.
- `light/sequences/*.json`: a start, events and expected outputs:
  - `settings`: the stored light settings at the start, as in the protocol (missing ones take their defaults).
  - `seed`: the seed of `twinkle`, 1 when missing.
  - `limit_mw`: a power limit other than the firmware's.
  - `events`: in time order, each with `at` (milliseconds since the start) and either `set` (settings changed as by `settings_set`) or `light` (`true` or `false`).
  - `expect`: each with `at`, `duties` (red, green, blue, white) and optionally `on`, `power_scale` and `timer_remaining_s`.

The light starts at time 0. The runner ticks every 10 ms, and also at an expected time off that grid. Events at a time apply before the output at that time is computed; an event between two ticks applies at the next tick.
